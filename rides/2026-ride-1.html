We tore through Spokane and Coeur d’Alene for four days, hunting twisties anywhere we could find them — around CdA and Harrison, through downtown Coeur d’Alene, between CdA and Spokane, and all over Spokane itself.

We also witnessed the devastating fire in Spokane, which shut down access to part of the north side and changed the riding plan. But the memory lane ride had to go on.
