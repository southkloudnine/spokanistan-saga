Back in June 2023, the saga made its way to Miami. Brother MAD was living there at the time, which meant local knowledge, warm weather, and immediate access to all kinds of questionable opportunities.

We spent three days riding through the city, visiting the infamous South Beach and a bunch of other landmarks, squeezing in a jetski ride, checking out Pablo Escobar’s place, and stopping by the house that showed up in Bad Boys 2. It was less about twisties and more about soaking up the city, making memories, and letting Miami do what Miami does.
