The original plan had everything: adventure bikes, Gulf Coast miles, Galveston, maybe a ferry, and the sort of unnecessary motorcycle expedition the Covenant surely intended. Then three grown men compared calendars and discovered we only have two days.

So the motorcycles have been benched. In their place: MAD’s Bimmer — climate control, cupholders, and every other humiliating luxury standing between us and greatness. We’ll pile in, turn up the music we all grew up listening to, and storm Galveston like three aging teenagers with better credit scores.

The revised mission is simple: beers, a swim, questionable coastal decisions, then back to MAD’s house to finish the trip at the pool. The pool is reportedly already occupied by frogs. We do not know how many, who invited them, or whether MAD has formally surrendered ownership. Diplomatic negotiations will begin upon arrival.
